Onboarding & offboarding

Onboarding controls

Create profile · assign role · record approved compensation · set effective date · verify payment setup · confirm access.

Offboarding controls

Confirm final date · reconcile earned pay · check outstanding commissions · review adjustments and refunds · verify payment settlement · archive profile.

No permanent deletion. Use archive, void, reverse, or reactivate with an audit trail.

Audit and governance concepts

  • Record who changed a value, when, why, and its effective date.
  • Preserve historical compensation rules and reviewed payroll snapshots.
  • Distinguish prepared, initiated, and settled payment states.
  • Use reversals and adjustments instead of deleting financial history.
  • Require role-based access and approval for sensitive changes.
These are design principles only; this prototype has no authentication, backend, or persistent audit service.
