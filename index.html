Onboarding

  1. Create profile and assign role
  2. Record approved compensation and effective date
  3. Set calculation basis and pay schedule
  4. Verify payment setup
  5. Complete access and equipment checks
  6. Confirm payroll eligibility

Offboarding

  1. Confirm final working date
  2. Reconcile unpaid salary and hours
  3. Check earned but unpaid commissions and bonuses
  4. Review refunds, clawbacks and adjustments
  5. Verify payment settlement
  6. Archive profile and preserve history
Final pay must show Outstanding, Under Review or No Outstanding Pay only after reconciliation.
